For restaurants

Plug-and-play: upload your PDF menu on the admin page, then paste one line on your site. The widget talks to whatever host it's loaded from, so there's nothing else to configure: 

<script src="https://your-host/widget.js"
        data-restaurant="demo"></script>

📤 Upload a menu (per restaurant)  ·  🔳 Generate table QR codes

Multi-tenant: test different restaurants

Each restaurant is a slug. Upload a menu under a slug on the admin page, then open the ordering page scoped to that slug — the QR codes do this automatically:

  • /?r=demo — the demo restaurant
  • /?r=seaside-grill — a second tenant
  • /?r=demo&t=12 — demo, table 12 pre-filled
